VEHICLE RUNTIME VI — 0.3.0

Target: GTA V Enhanced Story Mode / free roam.
Requires Script Hook V and Script Hook V .NET Enhanced v1.1.0.6 or compatible newer build.

Install:
- copy scripts/VehicleRuntimeVI.dll to GTA V\scripts\
- keep BattlEye disabled for script-modded Story Mode as usual

0.3.0 PHYSICAL VEHICLE INTERACTIONS
- attempting to enter a locked vehicle opens a two-direction theft wheel
- left chooses a quiet lock-working animation; right chooses GTA's physical
  smash-window task
- neither path contains a minigame, progress puzzle, text list or third-party asset
- hold Context while seated to open a six-direction vehicle wheel
- the wheel controls engine, seatbelt, driver window, locks, interior light and
  headlights; releasing Context executes the selected physical state change
- a fastened seatbelt blocks accidental exit/ejection behavior until deliberately
  released through the vehicle wheel
- vehicle control states persist per physical model/plate identity

0.2.0 CORRECTIONS
- merely standing beside a locked car never displays or loops a lockpick action
- pressing the normal vehicle-enter control on the intended locked car starts one
  bounded lockpick action
- the action is entirely physical: turn toward the lock, play Rockstar's existing
  parking-meter/lock-working animation sequence, unlock, then enter; there is no
  lockpick minigame, progress puzzle or 2D overlay
- successful access hands directly back to GTA's normal enter-vehicle task
- personal vehicles and already-running engines retain the 0.1.1 safety corrections

EFFECTIVE SYSTEMS
- ambient free-roam vehicles receive deterministic lock profiles
- protagonist personal-car model safety whitelist (Tailgater / Buffalo S / Bodhi)
- attempt to enter a locked vehicle to begin bypassing its lock
- higher lock tiers take longer and can trigger the vehicle alarm
- stolen/unkeyed vehicles do not start instantly: an automatic hotwire delay is applied after entry
- hotwired vehicles are remembered persistently
- deterministic tracker presence is stored per vehicle
- after stealing a tracked vehicle, hold Context near the rear of the car to disable the tracker
- active vehicle state is exported to scripts\VehicleRuntimeVI\ActiveVehicleState.txt for future Police/Core integration
- all persistent profiles live in scripts\VehicleRuntimeVI\VehicleProfiles.txt

MISSION SAFETY
The runtime yields completely when Rockstar's mission flag, cutscene, player switch,
disabled player control or screen transition is active, then waits five seconds before
resuming. Mission entities are never lock-managed, including a vehicle the player is
already sitting in.

This first runtime intentionally does not replace vanilla archives and does not modify RPF files.
