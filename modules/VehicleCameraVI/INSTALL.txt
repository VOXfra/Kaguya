Vehicle Camera VI 0.1.0
=======================

Adds subtle speed, acceleration, collision and cornering motion to the ordinary
GTA V vehicle gameplay camera without replacing it with a scripted camera.

Install:
- Copy scripts\VehicleCameraVI.dll and scripts\VehicleCameraVI.ini into scripts.
- Requires ScriptHookV and ScriptHookVDotNet Enhanced v1.1.0.6.

Design:
- Keeps the player's own mouse/stick camera movement.
- Small look-ahead offset is injected as a delta, not an absolute camera angle.
- Road vibration grows smoothly with speed.
- Acceleration/deceleration and collisions add short extra motion.
- Effects stop during cutscenes, character switching and free aiming.

All values are configurable in VehicleCameraVI.ini.
