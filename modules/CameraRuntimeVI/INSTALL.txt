CAMERA RUNTIME VI — 0.2.0

TARGET
- GTA V Enhanced Story Mode

INSTALL
1. REMOVE or disable VehicleCameraVI.dll / VehicleCameraVI.ini. VehicleCameraVI is deprecated and must never run beside CameraRuntimeVI.
2. Copy CameraRuntimeVI.dll and CameraRuntimeVI.ini into GTA V\scripts\.

DESIGN
- the player always owns camera direction
- the runtime never writes gameplay-camera relative heading/pitch or recenters the view
- only subtle secondary motion is added for walking/running/sprinting/falling, speed, acceleration and impacts
- collision feedback now triggers on a new impact edge with cooldown instead of repeatedly pulsing while the vehicle remains in contact
- amplitude transitions are smoothed without changing camera direction

STORY MODE SAFETY
The effect is removed immediately for:
- mission flag / cutscene
- protagonist switch
- loss of player control
- fades and screen transitions
- scripted/cinematic camera
- aiming/free aim
- deliberate manual camera movement and look-behind

After Rockstar returns control, the runtime waits five seconds before adding secondary motion again.

VehicleCameraVI is intentionally excluded from the complete overhaul package.
