WORLD LIFE VI — V0.1.0

TARGET
- GTA V Enhanced Story Mode

REQUIRES
- Script Hook V
- ScriptHookVDotNet Enhanced

INSTALL
1. Copy the contents of this package into the GTA V root directory.
2. WorldLifeVI.dll and WorldLifeVI.ini must end up in GTA V\scripts\.

V0.1 DYNAMIC WORLD PASS
- dynamic pedestrian/scenario-ped density refreshed every frame using GTA density natives
- city daytime/evening crowds are denser; deep night and rural areas are quieter
- beaches/busy urban zones can receive additional pedestrian density
- traffic increases are deliberately smaller than pedestrian increases
- local ped/vehicle budgets taper density back toward vanilla before the scene gets overloaded
- no population archive/gameconfig replacement is required
- optional Online civilian vehicle integration uses models already present in the installed game
- Online vehicles are selected by donor vehicle class (compact/sedan/SUV/muscle/sports/off-road etc.)
- weaponized/military/special vehicles are deliberately absent from the ambient pool
- replacements happen only at distance and outside player LOS, never to mission vehicles or the player's vehicle
- vehicles with passengers are not replaced in this first pass
- asynchronous model requests prevent a traffic integration check from blocking the game thread
- scripted/cutscene contexts yield to Rockstar

NOTES
- Online vehicle integration is intentionally low-frequency. It is meant to make the world less 2013-like, not turn every street into a GTA Online garage.
- Density and Online vehicle integration can be disabled independently in WorldLifeVI.ini.
- Runtime-only architecture: no vanilla archive is replaced.
