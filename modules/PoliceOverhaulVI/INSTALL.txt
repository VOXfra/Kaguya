POLICE OVERHAUL VI — V0.2.0

TARGET
- GTA V Enhanced Story Mode

REQUIRES
- Script Hook V compatible with the installed GTA V Enhanced build
- Script Hook V .NET Enhanced (SHVDNE) v1.1.0.6 or compatible newer build

INSTALL
1. Copy the contents of this package into the GTA V root directory.
2. PoliceOverhaulVI.dll and PoliceOverhaulVI.ini must end up in GTA V\scripts\.
3. When updating, overwrite BOTH DLL and INI.
4. Keep scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI\UI\ if you already installed the VI-style face/clothes/vehicle/star PNG assets.
5. Launch Story Mode with BattlEye disabled as required for script mods.

UNINSTALL
- Delete scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI.dll
- Delete scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI.ini
- Delete scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI\ to also remove logs, fine-mail archive and persistent police cases.

NO VANILLA ARCHIVE IS REPLACED.

V0.2 MAJOR CHANGES
- Traffic fines are no longer silent balance deductions: a native iFruit-style LSPD Traffic Division notice gives reason, speed/limit, location, amount paid and outstanding balance.
- A persistent scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI\TrafficMail.log archive records delivered traffic notices.
- Police-observed reckless speeding can request a one-star traffic stop; fixed cameras only issue remote citations.
- Search HUD is now a bounded live search phase rather than a direct reflection of persistent CaseMemory.Active.
- Search circles appear only after contact is lost / during the post-pursuit search and expand with uncertainty as the last-known position gets older.
- Player death immediately suppresses the prior search HUD while the internal police case can remain in memory.
- Historical evidence/warrants do not themselves authorize lethal force.
- One/two-star response remains non-lethal unless the player actually shoots or directly aims at law enforcement.
- One-star close compliant encounters can resolve into an arrest task instead of immediate combat.
- PIT authorization remains separate from lethal-force authorization.
- Tier-three custom dispatch is non-lethal interception support; tier four/five tactical units are lethal-ready; tier six is military.
- Rural tier-three support uses sheriff units where appropriate.
- Warrant stakeouts identify first and defer force decisions to the central Force Policy instead of aiming a gun immediately.

RETAINED SYSTEMS
- physical witnesses and delayed reporting
- CCTV reporting/re-identification
- face/outfit/weapon/suspect-count/vehicle/color/plate evidence
- ANPR-style plate matching and deterministic trackers
- persistent cases and warrants per protagonist
- home stakeouts
- sixth-star military escalation
- mission-safe passthrough

IMPORTANT
The phone fine notice intentionally uses stable native feed/message UI instead of writing directly into version-sensitive appemail global memory. It behaves like a phone-delivered official notice and the detailed archive persists on disk without risking cellphone state corruption after title updates.

For testing, delete scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI\PoliceOverhaulVI.log before a session and send the new log if a pursuit, search, traffic stop or fine behaves incorrectly.
