WORLD LIFE VI — 0.3.0

TARGET
- GTA V Enhanced Story Mode

REQUIRES
- Script Hook V
- ScriptHookVDotNet Enhanced

INSTALL
1. Copy the package contents into the GTA V root directory.
2. WorldLifeVI.dll and WorldLifeVI.ini must end up in GTA V\scripts\.

0.3.0 STORY-FIRST WORLD RHYTHMS
- moving traffic, parked vehicles, ambient pedestrians and scenario pedestrians now follow separate hourly rhythms
- morning and evening commuter traffic peaks independently from foot traffic
- scenario activity drops more strongly during deep night instead of being a copy of ambient ped density
- beaches, busy districts and rural zones keep distinct behaviour
- local performance budgets taper each population channel independently before scenes become overloaded
- Online civilian vehicles remain low-frequency and class-appropriate
- donor swaps happen at distance, outside player LOS, never to mission entities, law vehicles/drivers, passenger vehicles or the player's vehicle
- Online vehicle swaps are suspended while the player is wanted so pursuits are never cosmetically rewritten underneath PoliceOverhaulVI

STORY MODE SAFETY
- mission flag, cutscene, protagonist switch, loss of player control and fades suspend the runtime
- pending vehicle swaps are cancelled immediately when Rockstar owns the scene
- free-roam population ownership resumes only after a five-second grace period

The system is runtime-only: no population archive or gameconfig replacement is required.
