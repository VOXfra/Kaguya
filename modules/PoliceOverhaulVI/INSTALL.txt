POLICE OVERHAUL VI — V0.3.0

TARGET
- GTA V Enhanced Story Mode

REQUIRES
- Script Hook V compatible with the installed GTA V Enhanced build
- Script Hook V .NET Enhanced (SHVDNE) v1.1.0.6 or compatible newer build

INSTALL
1. Copy the contents of this package into the GTA V root directory.
2. PoliceOverhaulVI.dll and PoliceOverhaulVI.ini must end up in GTA V\scripts\.
3. When updating, overwrite BOTH DLL and INI.
4. Keep scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI\UI\ if you already installed the private VI-style face/clothes/vehicle/star PNG assets.
5. Launch Story Mode with BattlEye disabled as required for script mods.

UNINSTALL
- Delete scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI.dll
- Delete scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI.ini
- Delete scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI\ to also remove logs, camera registry, fine-mail archive and persistent police cases.

NO VANILLA ARCHIVE IS REPLACED.

V0.3 MAJOR CHANGES
- Police identity is confidence-based. Face, outfit and vehicle are separate evidence channels; seeing clothes or a known vehicle is not automatically proof of who is driving/wearing them.
- A real mask caps face confidence. Exposed hands/skin/body shape are descriptive only and never treated as unique biometric identity.
- High notoriety / Most Wanted status lowers the confirmation threshold, but never grants magical position or identity knowledge.
- Story heist scripts (Vangelico, Merryweather, Bureau and Union Depository variants) are monitored read-only during Rockstar mission passthrough; sufficiently long completed runs add historical notoriety without private-global offsets.
- Warrants/home stakeouts require confirmed identity rather than a single FaceKnown boolean.
- Voluntary surrender is available on foot at Mission Row LSPD, Sandy Shores Sheriff and Paleto Bay Sheriff: holster weapons and hold the context key near the station.
- Surrender clears the active wanted/warrant phase and reduces heat/notoriety slightly; archived evidence/history can remain.
- Fixed traffic-camera enforcement uses a persistent exact-position registry of prop_cctv_cam_* objects only. prop_cctv_pole_* is never considered a speed camera.
- The registry seeds known exact world positions, discovers the physical Enhanced props encountered in-game and writes scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI\TrafficCameras.csv.
- A camera must physically exist at the recorded coordinate, have line of sight and be traffic-validated by seeing a vehicle on-road before issuing a citation.
- Traffic notices include the camera identity when applicable.
- Dispatch can use GTA Online law-enforcement vehicle models when present, with vanilla fallbacks if a model is missing from the installed build.
- Tier 4/5 tactical units start in interception/containment mode; current threat + Force Policy controls lethal escalation.
- Civilian collateral risk now affects lethal fire and PIT authorization. High risk delays/withholds the action; an immediate lethal threat uses a higher emergency threshold rather than an absolute ban.
- Sixth-star explosive air response performs a civilian-risk check. High-risk scenes prefer police-helicopter overwatch/ground containment; jet attack is delayed until collateral risk is acceptable.

RETAINED SYSTEMS
- physical witnesses and delayed reporting
- CCTV reporting/re-identification
- weapon/suspect-count/vehicle/color/plate evidence
- ANPR-style plate matching and deterministic trackers
- persistent cases and warrants per protagonist
- home stakeouts
- sixth-star military escalation
- bounded search HUD + death cleanup
- proportional force / PIT separation
- iFruit-style traffic fine notices + TrafficMail.log
- mission-safe passthrough

IMPORTANT
- Compile success proves SHVDNE API compatibility, not every runtime behavior. Test actual pursuits, stations and camera positions in Enhanced.
- The phone fine notice intentionally uses stable native feed/message UI instead of direct writes into version-sensitive appemail global memory.
- Old Cases.xml files are migrated: old FaceKnown/OutfitKnown flags are converted to initial confidence values instead of discarded.

For testing, delete scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI\PoliceOverhaulVI.log before a session and send the new log if a pursuit, identification, surrender, radar, PIT or military response behaves incorrectly.
