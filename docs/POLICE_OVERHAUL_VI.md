# Police Overhaul VI

Police Overhaul VI is the police/crime module of the modular GTA V Enhanced -> GTA VI-style overhaul.

## Core rule
The simulation may know a lot. **The player is not told what the police knows.** No suspect-description HUD, plate status, search circle, police dots or magical radio feed. The player gets the wanted stars and must infer everything else from the world.

## V0.1 systems pass
- physical witnesses and delayed reporting
- civilian / police / CCTV observation sources
- face, clothing, weapon, suspect-count, vehicle, color and plate evidence
- separate person and vehicle recognition + ANPR-style plate recognition
- persistent cases and warrants per protagonist
- last-known-position search centering
- CCTV re-identification
- probabilistic OEM trackers + permanent police-vehicle trackers
- speeding enforcement from fixed cameras / observed police and delayed fines
- known-home stakeouts for active warrants
- custom tactical dispatch from three stars upward
- sixth-star military tier with heavy ground response, attack helicopter and jet
- mission-safe passthrough and custom-unit cleanup

V0.1 replaces no vanilla archive. Future Character, NPC, Wildlife/Hunting, Vehicle, Interaction and Environment modules stay optional and separate.
