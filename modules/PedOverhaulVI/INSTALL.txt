PED OVERHAUL VI — V0.3.1

TARGET
- GTA V Enhanced Story Mode

REQUIRES
- Script Hook V
- ScriptHookVDotNet Enhanced

INSTALL
1. Copy the contents of this package into the GTA V root directory.
2. PedOverhaulVI.dll and PedOverhaulVI.ini must end up in GTA V\scripts\.
3. When updating from 0.3.0, overwrite BOTH the DLL and INI; the 0.3.1 traffic tuning lives partly in the INI.

MODULARITY
- Police Overhaul VI is NOT required.
- If Police Overhaul VI is loaded, it owns law-enforcement peds while Ped Overhaul VI handles civilians/gangs/ambient NPCs.

V0.3.1 VEHICLE / FALSE-POSITIVE HOTFIX
- keeps the 0.3 shared scene-awareness runtime
- vehicle occupants are no longer given pedestrian/navmesh/cower/flee tasks
- ambient drivers and passengers stay under Rockstar vehicle AI for now instead of abandoning their cars because of a social/traffic cue
- stale pedestrian states are removed when a ped enters a vehicle, preventing CrowdFlight propagation from a driver
- vehicle-hazard trajectory thresholds are tightened so ordinary road traffic passing beside a pavement is much less likely to become a collision emergency
- vehicle hazard look-ahead and audible radius are reduced; imminent direct collision paths still trigger pedestrian evasion
- crowd-flight and stranger-warning confidence are reduced to prevent self-sustaining panic cascades
- a mask alone no longer accumulates threat suspicion indefinitely; mask + weapon remains meaningful through the combination bonus

V0.3 SHARED-SCENE-AWARENESS BASE
- attention / suspicion / certainty / fear cognitive model
- shared scene scanner for NPCs, vehicles, bodies, fire and explosions
- NPC-vs-NPC fights, external gunfire and visible hostile weapons
- social information propagation with imperfect confidence
- vehicle trajectory / closest-approach / time-to-impact awareness
- discreet withdrawal, watch, film, phone, cover, flee, surrender and morale behaviours
- personality-driven reactions and bounded processing

No vanilla archive is replaced.
