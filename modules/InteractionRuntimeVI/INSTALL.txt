INTERACTION RUNTIME VI — 0.2.1

TARGET
- GTA V Enhanced Story Mode

REQUIRES
- Script Hook V
- ScriptHookVDotNet Enhanced v1.1.0.6 or compatible newer build

INSTALL
- InteractionRuntimeVI.dll and InteractionRuntimeVI.ini go in GTA V\scripts\.
- Ped Overhaul VI is recommended but not mandatory.

CONTEXTUAL INTERACTION
- look at a nearby free-roam civilian first; target acquisition happens passively from the camera
- only when a stable eligible civilian is actually targeted can holding Context (E by default) become VOX Focus
- LEFT = friendly/calm intent
- UP = neutral/contextual intent
- RIGHT = antagonize/threaten intent
- the display is a tiny three-direction affordance, never a trainer-style action list

0.2.1 STORY/CONTROL CORRECTIONS
- E is no longer captured merely because it was pressed; without an eligible target, vanilla Context remains untouched
- entering a vehicle, aiming, missions and mission entities cannot be stolen by the interaction layer
- weapon wheel/weapon-slot controls are no longer globally suppressed while looking at a pedestrian
- target range/cone are tighter to avoid attaching to unrelated pedestrians
- mission flag, cutscene, protagonist switch, loss of player control and fades suspend the runtime, followed by a five-second grace period

There are no dialogue trees, relationship meters, phone apps or interaction menus. Ped cognition and ambient Rockstar speech decide the reaction.
