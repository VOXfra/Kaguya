VOX GTA V Enhanced -> VI - AIO RC4 Runtime Repair

This release is a regression-repair pass based on the RC3 in-game test.

Inventory / weapons
- Removed SatchelRuntimeScript and its custom weapon/satchel/craft wheels.
- Four carried weapon categories only: long gun, sidearm, melee, throwable.
- Loadout selection is performed at Ammu-Nation using GTA's native weapon wheel.
- Legacy SatchelWeapons.txt is disabled and never used to grant weapons.
- Molotov crafting is exposed while the Molotov is selected in the native wheel; there is no craft wheel/page.
- Trunk inventory only exists at the physical rear of a vehicle, opens the trunk door and permits exact item selection.
- Stored weapon snapshot includes ammo, tint and known components.
- Named outfits are vehicle-trunk local.
- General-store materials are checkout-local, never world-global.

Vehicle theft
- No vehicle interaction radial.
- A locked-car entry attempt opens a bounded method choice instead of auto-lockpicking.
- Hold Enter/Y for quiet lock work, or Attack/RT for window smash; Cancel aborts.
- Driver-door bone is used to calculate a single approach/animation anchor.
- Approach and break-in tasks are issued once per phase; holding a button cannot restart them every frame.
- Door is unlocked only after the chosen animation completes.

Traffic
- RC3 Online donor replacement is disabled for this repair release.
- The ongoing traffic-retask healer was removed.
- Vanilla traffic owns driving until an Online-vehicle transfer can preserve driver/task atomically.

Police
- Removed PursuitDriverRecoveryScript.
- ForcePolicySystem is advisory only and no longer rewrites Rockstar police weapons, combat tasks or driver tasks.
- Normal 1-5 star ground response is Rockstar-owned.
- Custom military response is reserved for tier 6.
- Custom tier-6 direct-combat units are removed when a real LOS search starts, so tunnels/walls can break contact.
- Player death clears native wanted, search state and all VOX-owned military entities, with post-respawn reacquisition grace.
- Field surrender added when on foot, unarmed, stationary and near a visible officer.
- Sixth-star display now calls Rockstar's own HUD_WANTED_STARS Scaleform and its built-in sixth star instead of drawing a shop/custom star.
- Search lifetimes are bounded by threat level.

NPC interaction
- State model changed toward RDR2-style contextual actions: Greet/Defuse, Antagonize/Threaten and Rob/Respond based on tension, fear/opinion and weapon state.
- No interaction ownership while aiming or entering a vehicle.

Fitness
- Three activities: chin-ups, bench press, free weights/barbell.
- Sessions detect/recover naturally ended scenarios instead of leaving the player frozen after a prop/animation ends.
- Fitness rewards differ by activity.
- Managed shoulder/upper-arm morph is driven by strength/lean-mass profile.

Tools
- Scan-RDR2-Install.bat and Scan-SoundPack.bat wrap the PowerShell scanners, show exit errors and pause before closing.

Not claimed as fixed until tested in GTA V Enhanced:
- runtime quality/positioning of every vehicle break-in animation,
- every protagonist clothing/accessory combination,
- every workout prop/scenario placement,
- exact six-star Scaleform runtime behavior on Enhanced,
- police driving/combat regression itself. The code paths that fought Rockstar AI have been removed; in-game test is authoritative.
