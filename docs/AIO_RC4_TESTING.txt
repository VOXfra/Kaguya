A. STARTUP / STORY SAFETY
- Start Story Mode and check ScriptHookVDotNet logs for load exceptions.
- Run a short mission/cutscene/character switch. VOX interactions, workout, inventory, theft and police ownership must yield immediately.
- Confirm VehicleCameraVI is not loaded.

B. POLICE REGRESSION - PRIORITY TEST
1. Create a normal 1-star witnessed crime in free roam.
2. Do NOT drive toward the police. Watch whether dispatched ground units actually arrive.
3. Escalate to 2-4 stars and drive normally through several intersections.
4. Observe several police cars: they must accelerate, steer and recover normally. They must not twitch forward as if a handbrake/task is repeatedly applied.
5. Exit the vehicle and fight. Rockstar officers must choose their own weapons/combat tasks and actually engage.
6. Check PoliceOverhaulVI.log: force messages should say advisory only; there must be no 'Retasked stalled police pursuit vehicle' entries.

C. SIXTH STAR / SEARCH / DEATH / SURRENDER
1. Sustain five stars and continued violent heat until tier 6 is authorized.
2. The first five stars must stay exactly Rockstar's native style; the sixth must come from the same HUD_WANTED_STARS artwork, not a shop/custom star.
3. Break direct LOS in a long tunnel/covered location. Tier-6 VOX military direct-combat entities should be withdrawn when custom search begins. They must not keep firing through walls/tunnel geometry.
4. Remain hidden until the bounded search expires. Six stars must be loseable.
5. During another wanted event, go on foot, holster the weapon, stop near a visible officer and hold Context. Surrender should clear the active chase.
6. During another tier-6 pursuit, die. After hospital respawn there must be no active wanted level or surviving VOX military unit, and the case must not instantly reacquire you.

D. IDENTIFICATION ICONS
- Outfit/vehicle icons should only display when those facts are currently known/valid.

- For weapon: expose a visible weapon to police at recognition distance; verify weapon icon appears.
- Change outfit/vehicle/mask and verify active signalment can invalidate independently.

E. INVENTORY / FOUR-SLOT LOADOUT
1. Visit Ammu-Nation with several legitimately owned weapons.
2. Enter loadout configuration and use the normal GTA weapon wheel to choose exactly one long gun, one pistol, one melee weapon and one throwable.

4. Confirm no weapon you never owned is granted. Check that legacy SatchelWeapons.txt was renamed .disabled if present.
5. Re-enter Ammu-Nation, change one slot, leave and verify only that category changes.

3. Hold the normal GTA weapon wheel and select Molotov.

5. Exactly one Molotov should be created and materials decremented. There must be no separate craft wheel/menu.

2. Press Context once. The trunk lid must physically open and the inventory must only exist while you remain at that trunk.
3. Store two different owned weapons one at a time. Holding Context must not repeatedly store/retrieve.
4. Reopen and choose exactly which stored weapon to retrieve. Verify ammo, tint, scope/light/suppressor components where applicable.
5. Save two outfits with different names, close/reopen trunk, choose the desired named outfit.
6. Walk away: trunk UI must close and lid should shut. There must be no 'coffre' entry accessible in the street away from a vehicle.
