INVENTORY RUNTIME VI — 0.3.0

Requires Script Hook V + Script Hook V .NET Enhanced.
Copy scripts/InventoryRuntimeVI.dll into GTA V\scripts\.

Physical trunk inventory:
- stand behind a non-mission vehicle
- with a weapon equipped, press H to physically store it in that vehicle's trunk
- while unarmed, press H to physically retrieve the most recently stored weapon
- the trunk opens and the protagonist performs a physical pickup animation before the transfer
- each physical vehicle has its own persistent five-weapon capacity
- ammunition is preserved; no weapon is silently deleted
- there is no inventory list, radial inventory menu or storage minigame

Mask affordance:
- while on foot in free roam, press M to remove the currently worn mask
- press M again to restore the last mask actually worn by that protagonist
- no clothing catalogue or accessory menu is created

Story Mode safety:
- mission entities are never used as storage
- missions, cutscenes, protagonist switches, fades and loss of player control suspend the runtime
- a five-second grace period prevents an interaction from firing as Rockstar returns control

The carried-weapon category limit remains deliberately disabled until a mission-safe migration path can preserve Rockstar-authored loadouts without blind weapon deletion.
