INTERACTION RUNTIME VI — V0.1.1

TARGET
- GTA V Enhanced Story Mode

REQUIRES
- Script Hook V
- ScriptHookVDotNet Enhanced v1.1.0.6 or compatible newer build

INSTALL
1. Copy package contents to the GTA V root.
2. InteractionRuntimeVI.dll and InteractionRuntimeVI.ini must end up in GTA V\scripts\.
3. Ped Overhaul VI 0.5.0 is recommended but not mandatory.

DEFAULT CONTROLS
- Hold E while looking at a nearby civilian to Focus.
- LEFT ARROW = friendly/calm intent
- UP ARROW = contextual/neutral intent
- RIGHT ARROW = antagonize/threaten intent
All controls are configurable in scripts\InteractionRuntimeVI.ini.

V0.1.1
- removes the 1/2/3 bindings that conflicted with GTA V weapon slots.
- sticky focus target with acquire hysteresis and a lost-target grace period.
- transient camera/LOS jitter no longer restarts the hold timer every scan.
- vanilla context/weapon-selection controls are suppressed only while Focus is held.
- RDR2-inspired hold-to-focus targeting without pausing the world.
- camera-cone + physical LOS target selection.
- target gradually looks back when focused.
- three contextual intentions change depending on whether the player is visibly armed and whether the target remembers fear.
- GTA V ambient speech banks are used; no RDR2 dialogue/audio is redistributed.
- local opinion/fear/recognition memory and Ped Overhaul bridge remain active.

This is still the first conversation layer. The immediate goal of 0.1.1 is stable targeting and controls before adding deeper dialogue trees.
