INVENTORY RUNTIME VI — 0.1.0

Requires Script Hook V + Script Hook V .NET Enhanced.
Copy scripts/InventoryRuntimeVI.dll into GTA V\scripts\.

Effective 0.1 systems:
- persistent weapon storage per physical vehicle trunk
- walk to the rear of a non-mission vehicle
- while armed, hold Reload to put the equipped weapon into that car's trunk
- while unarmed, hold Reload to recover the most recently stored weapon
- ammo count is preserved
- trunk contents persist in scripts\InventoryRuntimeVI\TrunkInventory.txt
- Rockstar mission/cutscene/player-switch ownership disables the runtime

This is the first physical-inventory layer. It deliberately avoids deleting excess weapons automatically until the carry-limit policy and UI are tested together.
