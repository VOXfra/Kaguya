INTERACTION RUNTIME VI — V0.1.0

TARGET
- GTA V Enhanced Story Mode

REQUIRES
- Script Hook V
- ScriptHookVDotNet Enhanced v1.1.0.6 or compatible newer build

INSTALL
1. Copy package contents to the GTA V root.
2. InteractionRuntimeVI.dll and InteractionRuntimeVI.ini must end up in GTA V\scripts\.
3. Ped Overhaul VI 0.5.0 is recommended but not mandatory.

DEFAULT CONTROLS
- Hold E while looking at a nearby civilian to Focus.
- 1 = friendly/calm intent
- 2 = contextual/neutral intent
- 3 = antagonize/threaten intent
All controls are configurable in scripts\InteractionRuntimeVI.ini.

V0.1
- RDR2-inspired hold-to-focus targeting without pausing the world.
- camera-cone + physical LOS target selection.
- the target gradually looks back when focused.
- three contextual intentions change depending on whether the player is visibly armed and whether the target remembers fear.
- GTA V ambient speech banks are used; no RDR2 dialogue/audio is redistributed.
- non-verbal responses and movement are preferred when a generic line is insufficient.
- local opinion/fear/recognition memory.
- reflection bridge to Ped Overhaul VI 0.5 so interactions can become part of pedestrian memory without hard assembly coupling.
- disabled during missions and active wanted response by default.

This is the first interaction pass, not the final conversation system. It intentionally avoids long synthetic conversations and preserves GTA V voice identity.
