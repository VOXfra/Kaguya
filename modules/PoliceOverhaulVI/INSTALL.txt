POLICE OVERHAUL VI — V0.5.0 TEST BUILD

TARGET
- GTA V Enhanced Story Mode

REQUIRES
- Script Hook V compatible with the installed GTA V Enhanced build
- Script Hook V .NET Enhanced (SHVDNE) v1.1.0.6 or compatible newer build

RECOMMENDED
- VOX Core VI 0.1.0. Police remains functional without it, but shared world events are then not persisted in the common VOX memory.

INSTALL
1. Copy the contents of this package into the GTA V root directory.
2. PoliceOverhaulVI.dll and PoliceOverhaulVI.ini must end up in GTA V\scripts\.
3. The package now also installs scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI\UI\ with original VOX HUD assets.
4. When updating, overwrite DLL and INI. Do NOT delete Cases.xml or CrimeScenes.xml unless you intentionally want a clean police-memory reset.
5. Launch Story Mode with BattlEye disabled as required for script mods.

NO VANILLA ARCHIVE IS REPLACED.

V0.5 SEARCH OWNERSHIP / ACTIVE SIGNALMENT
- A free-roam chase can now enter a Police Overhaul owned CustomSearch after sustained loss of direct police/CCTV contact.
- During CustomSearch, vanilla police are temporarily told to ignore the Player entity so a one-frame glimpse cannot magically reacquire the suspect.
- The wanted/search state remains visible while police search the retained last-known area.
- Reacquisition requires a continuous physical observation and a concrete current match. Losing LOS resets the confirmation timer.
- A one-frame glimpse between buildings can create a candidate but cannot confirm identity.
- Historical evidence and CURRENT signalment are separate.
- The current vehicle badge/search criterion becomes invalid if the suspect abandons or changes the flagged vehicle out of sight.
- The current outfit badge/search criterion becomes invalid if the suspect changes clothes out of sight.
- A changed mask descriptor becomes invalid rather than following the player magically.
- A genuinely identified face remains historical identity evidence; removing a mask does not erase a face the police already truly identified.
- Trackers locate the flagged VEHICLE. They update its last-known search position but no longer restore the driver's wanted level by themselves.
- Search duration scales strongly by threat: about 75 s at level 1, 90 s at 2, 120 s at 3, 160 s at 4, 220 s at 5 and at least 300 s at internal level 6.
- High-level search areas are district scale; level 6 is substantially larger than level 5.
- A safety-net runtime opens/binds a police case when Enhanced/vanilla raises a free-roam wanted state that the previous incident interception path missed.

HUD
- Original VOX face/clothes/vehicle/red-star PNG assets are now packaged with every clean install.
- Face is historical only after genuine identification.
- Clothes and vehicle represent current actionable signalment and disappear after an unseen change.

WORLD CONTINUITY RETAINED
- Serious incidents can create persistent crime-scene records in scripts\PoliceOverhaulVI\CrimeScenes.xml.
- Returning later can reveal a bounded investigation zone plus evidence markers.
- Crime scenes decay over configurable real-time hours according to severity.
- With VOX Core installed, crime scenes are also published to shared world memory.
- Merchant BOLO recognition requires a serious prior case, genuinely known face, exposed face, line of sight and delayed confirmation/reporting.

OTHER SYSTEMS RETAINED
- physical witnesses and delayed reporting
- CCTV reporting/re-identification
- ANPR-style plate matching and deterministic trackers
- persistent cases, warrants and home stakeouts
- proportional force / PIT separation and civilian-risk checks
- sixth-star military escalation
- traffic camera enforcement and vehicle-ownership-aware citations
- GTA Online police-vehicle support when models exist
- mission-safe passthrough: cutscenes/player switches always yield; sustained real mission ownership remains vanilla-first
- iFruit-style fine notification + persistent TrafficMail.log archive

KNOWN LIMITATION: VANILLA MAIL APP
- Arbitrary persistent text cannot safely be inserted into Story Mode appEmail through SHVDNE alone.
- FiveM/Cfx ADD_TEXT_ENTRY is not a GTA V native and is intentionally not used.
- Until an Enhanced-specific GXT2 text-bank/hook is packaged, traffic fines use the iFruit notification plus TrafficMail.log history.

KNOWN TEST-BUILD LIMITATIONS
- Compile success does not prove SET_POLICE_IGNORE_PLAYER completely suppresses every Rockstar/custom chase task in every situation. Runtime testing is required.
- Custom tactical units may need a dedicated last-known-position search task if Enhanced ignores the police-ignore flag for an already assigned explicit chase task.
- Crime-scene forensic officers, tape/barriers, coroners, EMS, tow trucks and staged cleanup are future lifecycle layers.
